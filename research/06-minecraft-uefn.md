# 06 Minecraft + UEFN

Date: 2026-09-07

## Minecraft Marketplace
- Bedrock only. Need Partner OK: 18+, tax ID, 3+ good works.
- Players pay Minecoins. Microsoft pays quarterly. Min $200. Late 30-60 days.
- Total paid to makers $500M+.
- You keep 70%. $3.99 sale = $2.79 to you.
- Prices: skins $1.99-3.99, worlds $3.99-7.99, textures $1.99-4.99, mashup $5.99-9.99.
- Levels: hobby $100-500/mo, part-time $500-2k/mo, full-time $2k-10k/mo, studio $10k-50k/mo.
- Tech: Blockbench, PNG, JSON, .mcpack, .mcworld. Blocky, must run on phone + console. Human check 2-12 weeks. Send seasonal 4-6 weeks early.
- AI: no clear ban. Must own all. No brands.
- Fall proof: Halloween sells 2-3x. Xmas 3-5x. Sale Oct 21-Nov 1 2025, 99 items 33% off. Spooky Season each Oct.

## Fortnite UEFN
- Need Island Creator: 18+, 30+ days old, 7 days edit in UEFN, tax, Hyperwallet.
- Pay monthly, 30 days late. Min $100 in 12 mo or $0.
- 3 cash streams: play pool + direct sales + 5% fan code.
- Pool = 40% of shop net. Epic keeps 60%. Paid $352M in 2024. $722M to 260k islands by Sep 2025. $1B+ by Jan 2026.
- Rate ~$50-150 per 1M mins. Base ~$67 per 1M.
- Direct sales to Dec 2026 you keep 100% (~74% cash). From Feb 2027 you keep 50% (~37% cash).
- 3,000+ islands earn more from sales than pool. 47% play is maker islands. 75M+ play islands in 2026.
- Props on Fab $5.99-189.99. Ex $49.99 env pack.
- Tech: UEFN + Verse. FBX + PNG. Max 100k memory or no publish. Check 30 min-48h. Need age rate. No stolen IP. Since Nov 1 2025 only paying players count.
- AI: yes but must label Created With AI. 41k AI spam got removed May 2025. Now tick + auto detect + daily limits.
- Fall proof: Fortnitemares Oct 9-Nov 1 2025. 14 outfits, 9 brands. New Nitemare Island + Demon Rush. Runs each Oct.
