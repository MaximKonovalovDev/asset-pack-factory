# 08 itch.io + Patreon + Ko-fi

Date: 2026-09-07

## itch.io cozy / horror packs + jams
- Small packs $3-10. Ex: $3.99 Halloween 78 sprites, $4.50 Cemetery 25 sprites, $6 horror bundle, $7 Haunted House.
- Big packs $10.99-19.99. Gloom $10.99 sale, Yokai $19.99.
- Fee open. You set 0-100%. Default 10%. Plus pay fee $0.30 + 2.9%.
- Sell at $2+. $1 sales lose 30% to $0.30 fee.
- Min pay $5. Wait 7 days.
- 30% of cash on itch is extra pay above min. Avg buyer pays +$1.50 extra.
- Autumn proof: Halloween Sale 2025 had 13,000+ on sale (2,300+ games + 5,800+ assets + 1,000+ tabletop). Ends Nov 4 11am PT.
- Jams: Scream Jam 2025 had 937 entries, 10,608 rates. Cozy Fall Jam yearly, 3-day. Spring 2025 had 370 people, ~100 games.

## Patreon + Ko-fi prop club
- Entry $3-10 works. Real clubs: $5/mo start, $12.50/mo cutters, 7.50 UK / 10 world print club.
- Full tiers $10-50. Ex 10/20/50.
- Patreon fee new pages after Aug 4 2025 is 10%. Old keep 8% Pro or 5% Founders. Plus 2.9% + $0.30. Under $3 legacy 5% + $0.10. Plus 2.5% if change cash. iOS Apple 30%.
- Ko-fi 0% on tips (Free). 5% on fans, shop, jobs. Plus ~3% + $0.30. Gold $12/mo for 0% fee. Break-even ~$240/mo.
- What sells: monthly drops, early access, sell-prints license, polls, vault.
- Autumn proof: Minifantasy Spooky Graveyard free on itch for Halloween, then only vault after. KayKit Oct 27 2025: 50% off tiers, 33% off bundles.
