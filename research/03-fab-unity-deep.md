# 03 Fab + Unity - Deep

Date: 2026-09-07

## Can small autumn packs sell? Yes.
- 60-80 props: $9.99-15.99 (73 props $9.99, 60+ props $15, 65 models $15.99)
- 150-180 props: $24.99-29.99 (182 props $29.99)
- Big bundle 1900 assets: $29.99
- Unity min price $4.99. Free allowed.

## Fees
- Fab: you keep 88%, Epic takes 12%. Real take less after card fees. $49.99 sale = ~$40.50-42.80 to you.
- Unity: you keep 70%, Unity takes 30%. $50 sale = $35 to you.
- No lock. Sell same pack on both.
- Payout: Unity PayPal monthly, bank $250 min quarterly. Fab ~$100 min, ~45 days.

## Tech rules
- Unity in: .fbx, .dae, .abc, .obj. 1 unit = 1 meter. Pivot at bottom, Z forward.
- Unity PBR: need 1+ map: color, normal, metal, rough. Photo size power of 2, .png/.tga.
- Unity LODs: use LOD Group. Demo scene must. Unity 6.5+ must work in URP or HDRP. Max 6 GB.
- Fab in: .fbx, .glb/.gltf, .obj, .usd/.usdz. Also UE5, Unity, UEFN native. Embed photos. Name like cube_basecolor.png.
- UEFN: low poly. UEFN makes 3 LODs auto (LOD = low detail for far). Nanite has fallback mesh. Photo max 2K, best 512.

## AI rules
- Fab: must tick Created with AI. In May 2025 one seller pushed 41,000+ AI assets and got removed. Buyers can hide AI. Wrong tag = down.
- Unity: AI allowed but must tell tool names + what you changed in AI field. Must clean mesh (retopo = rebuild low). No words like hand drawn. No mass spam.

## Season peak
- Peak Sep-Oct for Halloween/autumn. Unity Fall Sale and Fab Sept Flash up to 70% off.
- In 2025 event sales were up to 50% of month cash, flash +25% more.
- Launch in Aug-Sep. Join sales.

## Best for tiny AI team? Fab first, then Unity.
- Fab pays more. Unity reach bigger: 3.3M devs vs 1.5M Unreal.
- Review: Unity 2-4 weeks, Fab 2-6 weeks.
- Lane: AI mesh -> rebuild low -> clean UV -> PBR -> LODs -> test in UE + URP -> tag AI -> ship both.
