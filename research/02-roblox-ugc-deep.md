# 02 Roblox UGC - Deep

Date: 2026-09-07

## Can we sell decor?
- Yes for hats you wear. No for home decor.
- Valid slots: hats, hair, face, neck, shoulder, front, back, waist.
- Autumn trick: pumpkin hat, leaf backpack, fall scarf.

## Fees, cash
- Upload: 80 Robux per item + ID check
- Sell permit (advance, comes back from sales): hat 1,500 Robux, limited hat 13,000 Robux
- DevEx: 1 Robux = $0.0038. 30,000 Robux = $114.
- Min cash out: 30,000 Earned Robux + 30 day wait
- Cut: base you get 30%. Price 2x floor = you get 50%. Price 6x floor = you get 70%.
- In-game sale split: you 30%, game owner 40%, Roblox 30%.

## Price, volume
- sweet spot 75 Robux. Range 50-150.
- Hats avg 95 Robux. Hair 60-100. Layered clothes 100-250.
- Avg sales per item in 30 days: hats 210, hair 340, faces 520.
- Median maker < 5,000 Robux/mo.
- Math: 95 Robux hat at 30% = 28.5 Robux = $0.11. Need ~1,050 sales to cash out.

## Tech
- max 4,000 tris (tris = tiny faces). 1 mesh only.
- Must be closed, no holes.
- Photo max 1024 (docs say up to 2048 cap).
- AI mesh allowed. They check result, not tool. Must pass tech + mod check. No stolen brand.

## Fall proof
- Halloween items 3-5x sales jump in Oct.
- List by late Sept.
- Roblox Fall Picks: Pumpkin Patch, Campfire Stories, Harvest Feast.
- Halloween Spotlight 2025 had 34 games in one hub.
