## AI disclosure - must follow
- Fab: must set Created with AI if AI made it. NoAI tag to block AI data use. Rule Jan 24 2025.
- Sketchfab: from Dec 11 2025 all AI need CreatedWithAI label.
- Unity 1.6: must fill AI field. List tools + what you changed. No words drawn, hand drawn, painted for AI. No mass spam. Update May 20 2026.
- Steam Valve Jan 10 2024: must tell Pre-Gen (art/code/sound made before launch) and Live-Gen (AI makes while game runs + guards). Shows on store. Players can report.
- Roblox: you own risk even if AI made it. No brands, logos, stars. Pure AI has no copyright. No DMCA for AI lava alone. Mix AI + human edit = can protect. Removes copies (Aug 19 2026 removed dup meshes). AI-only cannot be licensed.
- SL: must own IP or have OK. No brands/stars. Linden follows DMCA. Repeat lose account. Group plan Aug 12 2024: no pure AI as main, label if 50%+ AI, no AI list pics.
