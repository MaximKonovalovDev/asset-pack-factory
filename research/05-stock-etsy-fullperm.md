# 05 Stock 3D + Etsy + SL Full-Perm

Date: 2026-09-07

## Stock stores
- Sketchfab Store closed Oct 2024. Sales moved to Fab.
- Fab: you keep 88%. Min pay $100.
- CGTrader: min $5. You keep 60% base, up to 85% top. Min pay $100.
- TurboSquid: min $2. You keep 40% base, 60% if exclusive. Min pay $100.
- Normal prop $5-50. Pro rig $50-200.

## Tech
- Files: FBX, OBJ, GLB, DAE, ABC, STL.
- Need clean quads (quads = square faces), no overlap UV, PBR maps: color, metal, rough, normal, AO.
- Games need LODs. Print needs watertight (no holes).

## AI rules
- Fab + Sketchfab: must tag CreatedWithAI. From Dec 11 2025 all AI models need label.
- TurboSquid: bans AI for sale. Must own full IP.
- CGTrader: has own AI tool. Must review. No raw 100 AI dump.

## Etsy digital
- Single STL $2-15. Pack $10-30.
- Fee: $0.20 per list for 4 mo + 6.5% + 3% + $0.25 US. Ads 12-15%.
- Files: STL, OBJ, FBX, GLB. Must be watertight + print guide.
- AI allowed. Pick Designed by seller. Write AI use. Must be your own design (rule June 10 2025).

## SL full-perm resell rule - key
- Never resell as-is as full-perm.
- Must add value. Make new build.
- Set next perms to no-copy OR no-transfer. Never full-perm again.
- No free gift. Many set min 50-100 L$.
- Tech: DAE + photos. Max 8 mats. Need 4 LODs + physics shape. Good prop 1-5 LI. PBR since 2023. glTF since 2025.
