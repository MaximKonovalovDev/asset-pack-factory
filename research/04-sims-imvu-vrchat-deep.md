Date: 2026-09-07

## Where cash flows
- Sims 4 CC: Patreon is main cash. Early access allowed, but must become free later.
- Sims CurseForge: official partner with EA. Free downloads. You earn from pool.
- Top Sims ex: Serinion Studio $2,155/mo, 394 paid fans.
- Old total: Sims Patreon $767,820 in 2017, $78,121 in Feb 2018 alone.
- IMVU: $0.40 per 1,000 Credits profit. 100 Credits profit = $0.04 per sale. PayPal only. 14 day wait.
- VRChat: no shop. Cash is direct. PayPal, Gumroad, Etsy, Fiverr.

## Price, fees
- Sims Patreon tiers $3-10, avg near $5. Patreon fee 8-12% + pay fee.
- CurseForge: $0.05 per point. 70% to makers, 30% to site. Small mods $1-20/mo. Popular $50-500+/mo. Top $1,000+/mo.
- IMVU: min pay $50, max $10,000 per pay. Cap over $500 uses 12-mo avg. Extra $5 fee after 2 pays in 30 days. Need VIP + Creator $9/mo. Q4 2025: 20% royalty cap per chain. Stickers no royalty.
- VRChat cheap: Fiverr base $100. Ready avatar $29.
- VRChat pro solo: base 500 EUR. Complex +200-500. VRM +150. Face track +250.
- VRChat studio: $890 / $1,990 / $3,490. Quest +$190. Outfit +$240. Source +$250. Time 1-3 months.

## Tech
- VRChat PC tris: Excellent 32k, Good 70k, over 70k = Very Poor.
- VRChat mobile tris: Excellent 7.5k, Good 10k, Medium 15k, Poor 20k.
- VRChat photos PC 40/75/110/150 MB. Mobile 10/18/25/40 MB.
- VRChat bones PC 75/150/256/400. Size PC max 200 MB down, 500 MB open. Android max 10 MB down, 40 MB open.
- VRChat rig must be humanoid (humanoid = human bones). Aim Good rank. Very Poor hides on Quest.
- Sims 4 tops LOD0: Low 0-7.5k, Med 7.5-15k, High 15-25k. Full outfit LOD0 Low 0-15k, Med 15-30k, High 30-50k.
- Sims safe: hair under 10k safe, over 30k very high. Clothes under 10k ideal.
- Sims photos: CAS 1024x2048, HQ mod 2048x4096, objects max 1024. Sides must be 4,8,16,32,64,128,256,512,1024.
- Sims files: must zip. Build/Buy and CAS use .package. Mods use .package + .ts4script.
- IMVU: made in IMVU Studio. Catalog check needed. Keep mesh low few thousand tris. Photos 256-1024.
- AI: CurseForge yes with clear note. IMVU/VRChat you must own rights. No stolen work.
